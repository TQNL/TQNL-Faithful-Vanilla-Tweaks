# Third-party assets and credits

This is an independent TQNL compilation and add-on. It is not an official
Faithful release or an official Minecraft product, and is not approved by or
associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

## Faithful

The compilation contains Faithful 32x artwork and the credited Faithful add-ons
listed in [report.txt](report.txt). The newer copper textures are derived from
the official Faithful 32x September 2026 release, with TQNL wax masks and statue
adaptations. The standalone 64x edition uses the matching official releases
recorded in its source provenance.

- Website: <https://faithfulpack.net/>
- Current license: <https://faithfulpack.net/license/>
- Unmodified license copies: [Faithful 32x V4](Faithful-32x-License-v4.txt) and
  [all pinned source licenses](visual-copper/source/licenses/).
- Per-asset copper source and artist credits: [source-provenance.json](source-provenance.json).
- Original compilation contributors and tweak inventory: [report.txt](report.txt).

These assets remain under their applicable Faithful terms, including attribution
and distribution requirements. The repository's MIT software license does not
replace these terms. Specific contributed add-ons retain their original terms.

## Vanilla Tweaks

Credits:
Vanilla Tweaks: https://vanillatweaks.net/

The compilation includes modified Vanilla Tweaks item models, appearance tweaks
and the complete original-derived Visual Waxed Copper Items tweak, including the
statue fixes. [Vanilla Tweaks' terms](https://vanillatweaks.net/terms/) apply to
these assets and their adaptations. The pack is distributed free of charge with
the required credit; the terms are not replaced by the MIT software license.
